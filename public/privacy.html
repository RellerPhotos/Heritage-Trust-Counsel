Privacy notice

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This notice describes how information may be handled when you visit this informational website or use its contact form. It is a draft for review by the firm before publication.

Please do not send confidential, financial, medical, account, or time-sensitive information through this website. Submitting a message does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, the website collects the name, email address, optional phone number, enquiry topic, and message you choose to provide. The form is intended for general initial enquiries only.

How an enquiry is handled

When the online form is configured, the information you submit is transmitted to the email delivery provider Resend so that it can be delivered to Heritage Trust Counsel at esqrobertmarrison@gmail.com. The website application does not write enquiry submissions to its own database. The resulting email may remain in the firm's mailbox and in service-provider systems under their applicable retention and security practices. The firm may use the information to review and respond to your enquiry.

Website and service providers

This website currently loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request when your device loads those resources. The website does not currently include advertising trackers or an analytics product. The hosting provider may process technical request logs to operate and secure the website.

Sharing and retention

Information submitted through the form is sent to the firm and its email delivery provider for the purpose of delivering and responding to the enquiry. The firm should establish its own retention, access, and deletion practices for enquiries and email. This draft does not establish a client-file retention policy.

Security and sensitive information

The contact form is not a secure document portal and should not be used to transmit confidential documents or sensitive personal information. If the firm needs documents, ask the office to provide an approved secure method.

Your questions

For questions about this notice or information you have submitted, contact the office at esqrobertmarrison@gmail.com or (909) 813-9207.

Notice review

This draft should be checked and approved by the firm, including confirmation of the actual hosting provider, email service configuration, applicable privacy obligations, and the firm's retention practices, before the website is made public.
